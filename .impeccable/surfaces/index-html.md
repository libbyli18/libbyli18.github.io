# Landing page (index.html)

Scope: the front page of libbyli.me. Mode: Experience. The globe page is photos.html; illustrations.html and about.html are quiet "coming soon" placeholders until Libby has content for them (no invented bio or work).

Audience: hiring managers and engineers judging craft; friends and family. Job: choose where to go. Constraints: stays in the site's muted nature world (Newsreader display, Hanken Grotesk body, slate ink, firefly cursor). Owner's references, round 2 (2026-11-22): two photographs, a hazy golden-hour valley of rolling grassland under stratocumulus with soft mountains in the haze, and a clear blue sky over a long gently sloping green field with a cumulus bank on the horizon. She asked for the art to look realistic like these, and for the words to be much smaller and off centre. This replaces the earlier drawn/illustrated rendition.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The menu is a landscape photograph you can touch. Three small words stand on the far edge of a real-looking field, like a fence line on the horizon. It refuses the centred hero, the nav bar and the illustrated look.

OWN-WORLD: Painted-by-light realism (GPU-painted today; generated raster plates would also fit): soft blue sky warming to hazy cream at the horizon; a lit cumulus bank and a grey-bellied stratocumulus band with sunlit tops; blue-grey mountains dissolving into haze; rolling grassland layers losing contrast with distance; a near field with perspective grass texture, light and shadow on its folds, pale seed heads in the foreground. Film grain. Slate words in Newsreader at small size. No boxes, no chrome.

STORY: The visitor takes in a quiet, believable field, finds the three small words on the horizon, hovers one and it hops up, and clicks through.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed, no scroll. Sky owns the top ~60%; cloud bank low behind the mountains, stratocumulus band in the upper sky. The near field's crest slopes gently up from left (~66%) to right (~57%). "photos  illustrations  about me" at ~20px sit on the crest in the right third of the screen (right edge near 86% width on desktop), baselines on the crest. Small wordmark top-left.

FORM: pinned by the owner (field, slanted hill, words on the horizon, hover float; now realistic, small, off centre); seed: none (pinned) — "refine the landing page art to make it look more realistic like these reference photos and make the category text much smaller and off center".
Signature interaction: hover or focus lifts a word on a springy overshoot, it bobs while held, and drops back with a little bounce. Motion grammar: one entrance (land settles back to front, words pop up one by one), clouds drift very slowly, gentle pointer parallax; reduced motion keeps only the lift.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
